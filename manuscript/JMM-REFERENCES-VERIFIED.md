# Verified reference seed for the JMM manuscript

This is a working evidence list, not the final bibliography. Each item below
has a DOI or an authoritative software/source record that was checked before
inclusion. A final manuscript may cite an item only where it supports the
specific sentence in question.

## Remaining bibliography gate

The final manuscript needs additional, directly relevant primary literature on
receptor preparation, docking validation, source-format provenance, and
reproducible computational workflows. Additions must be individually checked
for bibliographic accuracy and cited for a specific scientific purpose; this
list must not be padded merely to reach a numeric target.
