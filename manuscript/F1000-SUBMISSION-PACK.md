## Journal-selection constraint

F1000Research is not an appropriate destination for this version. Its current
policy prohibits generative AI in the creation or manipulation of code and
figures, whereas this project documents AI assistance in those activities.
The manuscript must be sent only to a journal whose policy permits transparent
AI disclosure. Elsevier's SoftwareX is a candidate to assess after the authors
review its current guide for authors, scope, and publication charge.
